### Notes

- Expressions are generated by a Python port of the Vitess generator loops; lists are read from the Go source and format strings are cross-checked against it on every run.
- Sampling keeps small families whole and splits the remaining budget evenly; within a family the expressions with the smallest SHA-256 of their id are chosen, so a larger target is a superset of a smaller one and ids never depend on the target.
- Families with a row schema (JSONExtract) insert their row into `vteval_test` as setup, as the Vitess MySQL comparison harness does.
